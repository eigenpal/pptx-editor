Insert a chart with **Insert** > **Chart** or `insertChart`. Change its type, title, legend, labels, axes, gridlines, and colors in the **Chart** tab or with `setChartType`, `setChartFormat`, and `setChartColor`, and read them with `editor.chartFormat()`. `requestPane` takes `{ focus: false }`.
