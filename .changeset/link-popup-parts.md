A click into linked text opens a link popup with Copy, Edit, and Remove (a read-only editor shows only the target and Copy), and `PptxEditor.HyperLink` and `useHyperlinkPopup` let you restyle, replace, or rebuild its parts; a `popups.hyperlink` renderer shows this reading mode only when registered with `definePopup(Component, { reading: true })`.
