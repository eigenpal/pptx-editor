Without the drawing module of `@pptx-editor.dev/pro`, Shape Effects can now remove a reflection, glow, or soft edge from the file. The menu offers No reflection, No glow, or No soft edges when the selected shapes or pictures have that effect, and No shadow also removes an inner shadow. The menu follows the selection. `chromeOfferedControl(slot, control, editor)` takes the editor to give these rows to a custom control.
