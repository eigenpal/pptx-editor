Add the slide master view (`setPresentationView` with `slideMaster`): edit the shapes, placeholders, and background of layouts and masters, and every slide on them updates. Open it from **View**, the status bar, or **Edit layout** on the slide. `SlideSummary` gains `template`, `name`, `usedBy`, and `masterId`.
