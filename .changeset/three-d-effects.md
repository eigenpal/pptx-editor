Draw 3-D shapes and preset shadows. The drawing module paints bevels, contours, and turned cameras with their extrusion in the editor and in PDF export, and blur in the editor. Preset shadows paint in every edition. Set and clear bevels and 3-D rotation from the Shape Effects menu in React and Vue, or with the `setShapeEffect` command, which also sets and clears blur.
