Edit the data of bar, column, line, area, pie, doughnut, radar, and scatter charts. `editor.chartData()` reads the series and categories, and `setChartData` changes names, labels, and values, and adds or removes series and categories. One edit updates the chart's caches, point counts, cell references, and point formatting, and writes the same cells into the embedded workbook when its structure allows it. **Edit data…** on the chart toolbar group, the **Chart Design** ribbon tab, and the chart context menu opens the new **Chart Data** tab of the side panel: a form with a card for each series, where each field applies on Enter or blur as one undo step. Saved archives also get the same entry times in every time zone.
