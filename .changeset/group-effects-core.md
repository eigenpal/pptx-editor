Draw group effects (`p:grpSpPr/a:effectLst`). A group's outer shadow paints as one shadow of all its shapes together, placed by the group's box. With the Pro drawing renderers, glow, inner shadow, soft edge, reflection, and blur also apply to the whole group. `ResolvedGroup` has a new `effects` field.
