Draw warped text (`a:prstTxWarp`) in the editor and in the PDF export: each glyph bends into the warp's paths. Set or remove a warp with the `setTextWarp` command or the Transform gallery of the new `Toolbar.TextEffects` control. The caret and the selection of warped text keep the flat layout.
