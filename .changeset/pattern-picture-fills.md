Fill shapes with a preset pattern (`setShapePattern`) or a stretched or tiled picture (`setShapePicture`), and set a pattern or tiled picture as the slide background. The fill menu adds **Pattern…** and **Picture…**, the Format Background dialog adds a pattern and a tile option, and the new `PatternDialog` shows the 54 patterns with their colors.
