The toolbar gains DOCX Editor's named parts (`Toolbar.Bold`, `Toolbar.FontFamily`, …), `Toolbar.Action` and `Toolbar.Separator`, group `priority`, `pinned`, `after`, `className`, and `overflowContent`, and `overflow={false}` to wrap the bar instead of using a More menu; the Vue toolbar gets the named parts, `ToolbarAction`, `ToolbarSeparator`, `after`, and `className`, and its bar wraps until it has a More menu.
