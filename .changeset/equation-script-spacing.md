Improve the horizontal spacing of equations. Scripts use the advances of the script-style forms of a math font and the space after a script of STIX Two Math. Radicals widen with their height, end with their radicand, and place a degree with the kerns of the font. Scripts of scripts draw at 60 % of the size, to the nearest half point.
