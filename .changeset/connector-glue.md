Drag the end of a straight, elbow, or curved connector near a shape to glue it to the shape's nearest connection site. The sites show while you drag. A glued connector follows the shape when it moves or resizes, and an elbow or curved connector routes again from site to site. `setTransforms` takes a `connector` route on a `TransformItem`, and `connectionSites` lists the sites of a geometry.
