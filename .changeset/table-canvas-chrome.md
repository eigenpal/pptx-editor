Tables now show editing chrome on the canvas. A handle at the top-left corner selects and moves the table. The grid line under the pointer is marked, and any table's lines drag. "+" buttons on the left and top edges insert a row or a column. The cell menu has Select table. The toolbar keeps a short table group on the bar while the selection is in a table.
