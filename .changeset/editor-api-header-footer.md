Add `Slide.headerFooter` and `Presentation.applyHeaderFooter()` to the editing API, as SDK extensions. They show or hide the slide number, the date, and the footer on one slide or on all slides, and can skip title slides. The automation protocol adds `readHeaderFooter` and `setHeaderFooter`.
