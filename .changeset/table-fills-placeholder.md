A new table or picture fills an empty placeholder: the one that is selected or holds the caret, or else the first on the slide. It keeps the placeholder's position, shape ID, name, and index. A table fills a content or table placeholder at the placeholder's width. A picture sits whole in a content placeholder, or fills a picture placeholder and is cropped to its shape. In the editor, empty placeholders show a dotted outline, and empty content, table, and picture placeholders show **Table** and **Picture** buttons.
