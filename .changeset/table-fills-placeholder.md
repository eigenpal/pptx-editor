A new table or picture fills an empty placeholder: the one that is selected or holds the caret, or else the first on the slide. It keeps the placeholder's position, name, and index, and animations and connectors that named the placeholder name the new object. A table fills a content or table placeholder at the placeholder's width. A picture sits whole in a content placeholder, or fills a picture placeholder, cropped to its proportions and in its geometry. In the editor, empty placeholders show a dotted outline, and empty content, table, and picture placeholders show **Table** and **Picture** buttons.
